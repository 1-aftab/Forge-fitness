A gym that takes training seriously, not itself.

FORGE opened in 2014 with one rack, a few kettlebells and a simple idea: good coaching should not be a luxury. Today we run a full strength floor, a conditioning studio and small-group classes for every level.

Whether you are lifting your first barbell or peaking for a meet, you get a plan, a coach who knows your name and equipment that is always maintained.

  • Free movement assessment for every new member
  • Coaches on the floor at every opening hour
  • No joining fee, no hidden extras
